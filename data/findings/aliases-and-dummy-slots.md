# Renaming shells, True stubs, and unread assumption bundles

The live kernel export contains `43,135` single-node project theorem forwarding wrappers (`38,194` under `GlobalTheory.T.*` and `4,941` across other namespaces), literal `True` placeholders wired into live hypothesis bundles, `3` high-cone misnamed theorems, and entire subtrees pulled into the proof solely to populate unread struct fields. None of these breaks soundness—under Lean's kernel, ignoring an assumption or proving `True` only makes the consumer do the real work—but they make file names, theorem names, and hypothesis counts unreliable proxies for mathematical content.

## Renaming shells and misnamed theorems

Of `38,677` live theorems in namespace `GlobalTheory.T.*` (`14,362` inside Pillar A alone), **`38,194` (`98.75%`) have compiled proof-term size `value_nodes = 1`** (a single expression-DAG node forwarding to an internal theorem under a different, often more classical-sounding name). Outside `GlobalTheory.T.*`, other project namespaces hold another **`4,941` `value_nodes = 1` theorem aliases** (`GlobalTheory.CompanionTheory` `670`, `Formalism.FourierDecayAlt` `451`, `GroundTheory.Heights₂` `407`, `Formalism.L2` `359`, `UpperTheory.Gal` `311`, `LateralTheory.Fin` `274`) plus `126` `def`s.
