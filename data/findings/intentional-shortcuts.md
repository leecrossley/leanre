# Intentional shortcuts: junkEx, AwayB.tame0, toy D7, and dropped hypotheses

Rather than formalizing unrestricted local Langlands for $\mathrm{GL}_n$ and full Frobenius local–global compatibility at every prime, the proof trims both waist contracts—[`LawsT`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55) and [`AwayB`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TypeConditions/WeightsConjBis.lean#L26)—to the depth-zero, tame, large-residue-prime cases that downstream steps consume. Full `dom >= 1,000` auditing also surfaces a live toy `D7` witness and six high-`dom` engines that ignore declared premises.

## Dummy fallback junkEx inside recTExists

[`recTExists`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/RecTExists.lean#L20) (`595` dominated decls) supplies a map `rc : IrrGL (w.adicCompletion E) → WD h` satisfying [`LawsT`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L55). Compared with classical local Langlands, `LawsT` omits $\varepsilon$-factors and global surjectivity, adds an explicit tame-type law ([`TypeLaw`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L35)), and guards `twist`, `central`, `dual`, `inj`, and `depth` behind `Good`—cuspidal support belonging to [`GoodCusp`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/IsRecTOfIsRec.lean#L20) (rank $1$, or smooth-character twists of depth-zero cuspidals over $\mathbb{Q}_g$).

Inside [`cuspEx`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/CuspEx.lean#L168), the cuspidal map branches on an [`if GoodCusp` split](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/CuspEx.lean#L177):

- On `GoodCusp x`, `r₀ x` computes actual $\mathrm{GL}_1$ local class field theory (via `artEx`) and Frobenius-induced tame depth-zero Weil representations over $\mathbb{Q}_g$ matching Green's regular characters.
- On `¬ GoodCusp x` (rank $n \ge 2$ of positive depth or over non-$\mathbb{Q}_g$ completions), it falls back to [`junkEx`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/LocalLanglands/LocalCentralUnits/JunkEx.lean#L257) (`55` decls), returning a single wildly ramified placeholder `J n` (`¬ IsTameWD`) twisted by an unramified character only to satisfy `absCentral`. The map is not injective on `¬ GoodCusp`.

Downstream consumers never touch that fallback. Every call site in `Row0b` inspects `rc (algNorm σ)` only under `Tor ∧ mono0` or `IsQpBig ∧ TmW ∧ Sqf ∧ mono0`: [`plainTor_of_tor`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L97) forces every cuspidal constituent to have rank `1` (`GoodCusp`), and [`mixed_of_tame`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L151) invokes [`dz_of_cusp`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/IntegralStructures/InertCharpoly/CharPolOfInert.lean#L114)—a finite-set pigeonhole on tame labels—to prove depth zero (`DZ`), ruling out wildly ramified `junkEx`.

## Effect on soundness

None of these shortcuts creates a soundness hole: because [`everyCharacter`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/MainConclusion.lean#L10) closes the implication from `Row0b` to `EveryCharacter` inside the Lean kernel, weakening an intermediate contract or dropping an unused hypothesis only shifts work onto the consumer, which must complete the proof using those narrower invariants alone.
