# Core mathematical components grounded in Mathlib types

Beneath the renaming shells and dead assumption bundles, the proof's load-bearing interfaces unfold directly to Mathlib fields, schemes, adeles, and Galois cohomology (see [the 60-theorem classical index](../analysis/famous_theorems_index.md) for scope ratings across all pillars). Deep tactic bodies beyond top-level statements and wiring remain unread by humans; what follows is what the formal types, kernel edges, and second-pass literature checks actually establish.

## Windowed Sha-length bound (VWin)

[`VWin`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L49) unfolds completely to Mathlib primitives (`0` opaque leaves in its statement):

```lean
def VWin : Prop := ∀ ψ : PChar, VWinAt ψ 401
```

Through [`VWinAt`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L44), [`WinPair`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L27), [`IsChiField`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L19), and [`VVle`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean#L12), it states that for every primitive Dirichlet character $\psi$, every $\varepsilon \in (0, 1/52)$, and every admissible prime pair $(q, d)$ ($q \equiv 3 \pmod 8$, $d \equiv 7 \pmod{16}$), there is a multiplicative window $[Y, Y^2]$ over which the abelian CM composita $E = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_{2^{e(x)+2}})^+ \cdot K_\psi \cdot \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-q}) \cdot \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-d})$ satisfy $\sum_{l \in S} \log l \sum_{1 \le e < \lfloor x^{401} \rfloor} \operatorname{length}_{\mathbb{Z}_l}(\mathrm{Ш}^1(G_{E, S_l}, \mathbb{Q}_l/\mathbb{Z}_l(-e))) \le \exp(x^\varepsilon / 2)$. The cohomology terms [`LData`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L54), [`GS`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/ReciprocityTheory/GaloisValuations/Global.lean#L141), [`Sha`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L75), and [`shaLen`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GaloisCohomology/Statement.lean#L78) are genuine continuous Galois cohomology built directly from Mathlib `CyclotomicField`, `jacobiSym`, `ValuationSubring`, `PadicInt`, continuous 1-cocycles, and `Module.length` (`401 = 1 + 400` is a consumer rescaling margin and the $\frac{1}{2}$ exponent is elementary $4\varepsilon/5 \to \varepsilon$ slack immediately weakened in `divinN_of_hypV`; see [the `VWin` audit](../analysis/audit_vwin_shafarevich.md)).
