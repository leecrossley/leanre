## 0. Findings
1. **The nine producers are one supply chain plus transports, not nine alternatives `[K][L]`.** Five form a linear chain ending in a wrapper with six callers; one is a localized restatement; three move the proposition across a change of group copy. All nine are live.
2. **The bundle-witness edge is fed by a tenth supplier that the producer query does not list `[L][K]`.** [The multiplicative-closure lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/MultiplicativeClosurePrimeMem.lean#L18) calls the wrapper and returns [a multiplicity condition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/MultQ.lean#L53) that hides `MultEq` under an eventual quantifier; its alias (fan-in 2) is the argument that reaches the bundle-witness premise on both routes.
3. **The theta-lift edge is fed by the localized restatement `[L][K]`.** [The local multiplicity lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/LocalTypeDictionary/TwistedLocalMultiplicityEq.lean#L13) fills one field of a hypothesis record; the lower-unitary-case lemma reads that field to discharge the multiplicity premise of the closing statement that the theta-lift theorem serves. The theta-lift theorem itself never sees `MultEq`.
4. **The statement is an equality of cardinals between two unitary groups identified at the finite adeles `[S][L]`.** Odd rank, a regular weight gap $100r^{4}$ and a level condition on $\sigma$ are assumed; finiteness of both sides is proved and used, so the equality is not an artifact of truncating infinite cardinals. No twisting automorphism occurs in any statement read, despite the name.
5. **Ten published inputs in form; none is open on the live path `[L][K]`.** Two are discarded by the assembly; eight are consumed. At the single construction site all ten are supplied by adapters whose open-literature argument is unused, directly or one level down. The open-literature record has three fields and none is one of the ten.
6. **Hygiene: no finding changes a conclusion `[L]`.** Six literal `True` slots in the spectral side, two discarded published inputs, and one unused equation hypothesis ($\mathrm{ccK}=\top$).
7. **Kernel `[K]`.** Every audited cone has exactly the three standard axioms, `sorryAx=0`, no `ofReduceBool`, no project opaque; the cone of the top producer contains no `decide_kernel` source.

## 1. What is stated `[S]`
### 1.1 The proposition
[MultEq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/FinRepApply.lean#L118) for a real datum $R$: for every index $i<m$, every discrete-series model $M_0$ on $G_0=U(Y_0)$, every matrix $j$ and topological isomorphism $\Phi:G_0(\mathbb A_f)\to G_i(\mathbb A_f)$, $G_i=U(W_i)$, tied by [a finite-adelic isometry record](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/FinRepApply.lean#L73) (Gram matrices matched by $j$, $\Phi$ is conjugation by $j$, frames matched up to an integral unit that is trivial at a fixed finite set of places), and every irreducible admissible $\sigma$ of $G_0(\mathbb A_f)$ satisfying [a level condition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/FinRepApply.lean#L109) (a non-zero vector fixed by a level subgroup and by a finite-group averaging operator),

$$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(\sigma,\ \mathcal A_\theta(M_0)\bigr)\;=\;\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{Hom}\bigl(\sigma\circ\Phi^{-1},\ \mathcal A_\theta(M_{W_i})\bigr).$$

Both sides are values of [one multiplicity function](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/FinRepApply.lean#L63) with values in `Cardinal`; $\mathcal A_\theta(M)$ is a space of maps from model vectors to functions on $G(\mathbb A)$ with right translation by $G(\mathbb A_f)$. Reading it as "cuspidal multiplicity" rests on a bridge lemma (`dom` 78, `UNREAD`) that rewrites each side as [the multiplicity of $\sigma\otimes M$ in the cuspidal $L^{2}$ space](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Foundations/MemL2OfAutomorphicEmbedding.lean#L103), assuming three of the published inputs.

### 3.2 The comparison `[S][L]`
[The comparison proposition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TraceEqXOfGeomExpansionX.lean#L353): for Hermitian spaces $V,V'$ of equal odd rank $r$, weights with gap, discrete-series models $M,M'$ with the same weights, a type with no fixed vector, local isomorphisms $j_v$ conjugating the standard lattices at every finite place and $\Phi$ restricting to them, $m_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\sigma,M)=m_{\mathrm{cusp}}(\sigma\circ\Phi^{-1},M')$ for irreducible admissible $\sigma$ carrying the type. [Its proof](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TraceEqXOfGeomExpansionX.lean#L366) (read in full):
1. choose a split auxiliary place outside the type, unit-normalized local Haar measures, and archimedean test functions from [local pseudo-coefficients](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/RangeShape.lean#L36) on both sides;
2. [spectral finiteness](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/HasTypeVec.lean#L50) gives $m_{\mathrm{cusp}}<\aleph_0$ and, per open compact level, a finite list of irreducibles with non-zero multiplicity; the two lists and $\sigma$ are merged into one family $\tau_1,\dots,\tau_n$;
3. for every finite test function $f_f$ of the given type, [the spectral trace formula](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/TestFnConstTermVanishX.lean#L244) on each side and the trace identity give, with $c_i=\operatorname{tr}\tau_i(f_f)$,

   $$\sum_i m_i\,c_i\;=\;\operatorname{tr}R(f_\infty\otimes f_f)\;=\;\operatorname{tr}R'(f'_\infty\otimes f_f^{\,j})\;=\;\sum_i m'_i\,c_i;$$

4. [the separation property](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/Divisors/HasTypeVec.lean#L73) (the trace functionals of the family are independent on such test functions) gives $m_{i_0}=m'_{i_0}$ in $\mathbb N$, and step 2 lifts this to cardinals.

The trace identity has [a short proof](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TraceEqXOfGeomExpansionX.lean#L301) (read in full) from an elliptic expansion that is the same finite sum on both sides `[M]`: $\operatorname{tr}R(f)=\sum_{P}\operatorname{vol}(T_P)\prod_{v<\infty}\Phi_v(P,f_v)\prod_{\rho}\Phi_{\mathbb R}(P,\lambda_\rho)$ over regular elliptic characteristic polynomials $P$; the real factors depend only on the weights and the finite factors are preserved by a lattice isometry. [The trace-comparison lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TraceEqXOfGeomExpansionX.lean#L537) instantiates $V=Y_0$, $V'=W_i$, takes the weights as integer parts of the datum's parameters ([parameter matching](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/TraceEqXOfGeomExpansionX.lean#L502): odd $r$, gap from the record), gets the type from the level condition using the Green input, and converts with the bridge lemma.
