# Frame Order, the Fifth Power of `m`, and the Bundle Witness: Mathematical Audit

> **Target & Scope:** Statement- and proof-body trace at `40e56bab` + `kernel.db` (`dom` `tree_id=0`). Tags: `[K]` kernel graph, `[S]` statement/def text, `[L]` proof-body lines read, `[M]` mathematical reconstruction; anything untagged `[L]` has an `UNREAD` proof body (§5; see [Scope & Tag Legend](README.md)).
> **Question.** The existence audit (`audit_stageu_existence_math.md`) left three items `UNREAD`: (1) where a frame of order $n\asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$ and its host field come from; (2) which analytic step needs $m^{5}$ in the lower row $x^{\varepsilon/100}\,m^{5}fL\,x^{\varepsilon}\le k$; (3) what object the single bundle-constructing proof builds, and what the closing lemma of the four-stage construction discharges.

## 0. Findings

1. **The frame is an explicit 2-power cyclotomic tower `[S][L]`.** A frame is a finite abelian group $A$ of order $n$ with a surjection onto $(\mathbb Z/2^{e+2})^{\times}/\{\pm1\}$; [fieldChi](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/FieldChi.lean#L20) builds it with $n=c\cdot2^{e}$ ($c\ge1$; $c\le\operatorname{ord}\chi$ by [ordAny](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/OrdAny.lean#L86)) together with an abelian CM field $E\subset\mathbb Q(\zeta_{Q_Sqd})$ of degree $4n$. The order $n\asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$ is obtained by choosing the tower height $e$, not by a density theorem. In the statements read, analytic input enters through the two auxiliary primes $q,d$, the host-goodness step and the window hypothesis `[M]`.
2. **$m^{5}=m^{2}\cdot m\cdot m^{2}$, born in a one-place weighted orbital bound `[L][M]`.** At each archimedean place the weighted bound loses $b^{Cm^{5}}$: a per-factor loss $b^{O(m^{2})}$, raised to a factor count $\le(11+A_w)m^{3}$ that is ($m/2$ leading-minor indices) $\times$ (weight exponent $A_w(m/2)^{2}$); one further term enters with $m^{5}$ already in a hypothesis (`UNREAD`). The product over the $f=2n$ places gives $e^{Cm^{5}f\log b}$ with $\log b=L=x^{\varepsilon}$.
3. **It is read by one decisive inequality `[L]`.** Non-generic rows carry $2^{-(k-m-3)}e^{C_nm^{5}f\log b}$; the row $8C_nm^{5}f\log b\le k$ is exactly what makes this $\le e^{-k/5}\le b^{-14}$. Two further readers are a definitional absorption ($\log\Gamma_P=C_\Gamma m^{5}fL$) and a weak one that would survive with $m^{3}$ (§2.3).
4. **The bundle witness is a tie-record around an automorphic evaluation map on an adelic unitary group `[S][L]`.** [bwit_exists_alt](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/BwitExists.lean#L53) receives the reciprocity record and the spectral data as inputs and adds a selected realization: a conjugate-linear map, isometric up to an explicit normalization, from the space of a discrete-series model into $L^{2}$ of an adelic quotient, given by what reads as matrix-coefficient evaluation `[M]`. It is not a Galois-cohomological surrogate, and the doubling-scalar injectivity engine lies outside its cone `[K]` (§3.4).
5. **The closing lemma is a one-line assembly `[L]`.** [memberE_closing_alt_alt](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/MemberEClosing.lean#L17) fills nine of the twelve premises of the second-pass theorem and passes the remaining three, plus two level-three conditions and the law relating the two norm-choice extensions, to its caller (§3.5).
6. **No degenerate witness found `[K][S]`;** three caveats are recorded in §4: every statement of §1 is conditional on the window hypothesis; the evaluation map has a zero default branch; several fields of the real input are conditional implications.

## 2. Why the Lower Row Carries $m^{5}$

### 2.1 Birth: the weighted bound at one place `[L]`
[The rotated weighted bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/CuspSpanNormBounds/WeightedUniformRotatedOfExpansion.lean#L31) says, for Harish-Chandra parameters with all gaps $\ge C_gm^{4}$ (here $m=2r_S+2$, [rS](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalRingCardinality/Params.lean#L38) $=m/2-1$):

$$\sum_{\gamma}\ \mathrm{normWt}(\gamma)^{A_w(m/2)^{2}}\cdot(\text{orbital term})\ \le\ \exp\!\big(C_b\,m^{5}\,|\mathrm{Tau}|\,\log b\big)\cdot J .$$

It follows ([product over places](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/CuspSpanNormBounds/WeightedUniformRotatedOfExpansion.lean#L49)) from a per-place bound with $b^{C_Km^{e}}$ at $e=5$ ([OnePlace](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/CuspSpanNormBounds/BesselShellMomentBounds.lean#L815)). The exponent is assembled in [weighted_uniform_rotated_of_expansion_alt_alt](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/WeightedUniformRotatedOfExpansion.lean#L295) (`dom` 273), with $M=m$:

$$\Big(2\,b^{(3+C_h)M^{2}}\Big)^{2\sum_lN_Q(l)}\le b^{(4+C_h)\,2(11+A_w)\,M^{5}},\qquad \sum_{l<M/2}N_Q(l)\le(11+A_w)\,M^{3}.$$

Three further terms have native size $b^{O(M^{2})\cdot O(M^{3})}$, $b^{O(M^{2})}$ and $b^{O(M^{4})}$ and are rounded up to $M^{5}$ (the middle one is the frame bound); one term $b^{CM^{5}}$ comes from a local weighted bound taken as a hypothesis there, whose statement already carries $m^{5}$ and a count $\le C_am^{3}$ (`UNREAD` upstream).

So `[M]`, for the terms assembled here, $5=2+1+2$: quadratic size of one local factor, linear number of minors, quadratic weight power. No Cauchy–Schwarz, lattice-dimension or doubling step is involved at this point.

### 3.4 Verdict `[M]`
The witness is a *typed record of compatibility ties around a concrete automorphic realization on an adelic unitary group*: a normalized conjugate-linear embedding of a discrete-series model space into $L^{2}$ automorphic functions by matrix-coefficient evaluation, bound by equalities to (i) the Weil-representation character and reciprocity record it is given, (ii) column data over explicit number fields, (iii) standard Hermitian lattices. It is not an abstract Galois-cohomological or Hecke-algebra surrogate: the only arithmetic inputs in the signature are Artin reciprocity, ray class fields and local reciprocity maps. Nor does it read as the place where a theta correspondence is proved: the Weil representation is an assumed input, the doubling-scalar injectivity engine is outside both its dominator subtree and its cone `[K]` (one declaration tested), and the multiplicity transfer that a theta correspondence would give is an explicit hypothesis (`MultEq`). The bundle is therefore best read as the *bookkeeping interface* through which a later stage consumes the evaluation map, not as the place where the correspondence is proved.
