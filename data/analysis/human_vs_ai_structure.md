## Key Empirical Findings

1. **Two distinct AI structural regimes (`Alpöge–Claude` vs. `OpenAI`), both sharply separated from human Mathlib-style libraries:**
   - **Comment and docstring bifurcation:** All four human repositories carry `7.67%`–`16.87%` comment-bearing lines and `74.02%`–`98.63%` module/declaration docstring file coverage. Both `riemann-hypothesis` (`17,374,215` LOC) and `navier-stokes` (`616,031` LOC) contain **`0` comment lines (`0.00%`)** across `177,457` combined files, whereas `NavierStokesAndEuler` (`641,332` LOC) carries `32,455` comment lines (`5.06%`) and `/-!` / `/--` docstrings in `2,642 / 2,659` files (`99.36%`).
   - **Keyword and naming stylometry (`lemma` vs. `theorem` and `name_compress_ratio`):** Human formalizations write `lemma` for `51.50%`–`85.49%` of all theorem/lemma declarations (`85.49%` in `carleson`, `65.37%` in `FLT`) and exhibit higher declaration-name entropy (`name_compress_ratio` `0.1702`–`0.1988`). All three AI megaproofs overwhelmingly emit `theorem` (`lemma` share `4.23%` in `riemann-hypothesis`, `6.02%` in `navier-stokes`, and `0.07%` — just `28 / 38,531` — in `NavierStokesAndEuler`) and compress more tightly (`0.1541` in `riemann-hypothesis`, `0.1126` in `navier-stokes`, `0.1173` in `NavierStokesAndEuler`), reflecting repetitive namespace prefixes and templated auxiliary naming.
   - **Micro-file sharding vs. monolithic outliers:** `riemann-hypothesis` shards proofs into `175,187` files of median length `58.0` LOC (`45,047` files — `25.71%` — contain **strictly `1` theorem and `0` other declarations**; `78,866` files — `45.02%` — contain `1` declaration of any kind). Neither Navier–Stokes repository uses `1`-theorem micro-sharding (`0.75%` and `0.90%`), yet both AI PDE proofs produce extreme single-file outliers (`20,755` LOC in [`Euler/EulerProof.lean`](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/Euler/EulerProof.lean#L1) and `7,086` LOC in [`lean/CellLayerChallenge.lean`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/CellLayerChallenge.lean#L1), vs. human maxima of `1,225`–`6,478` LOC).
2. **Tactic discipline, linter enforcement, and heartbeat / kernel pressure:**
   - **Forward `have` chains vs. algebraic/congruence automation:** AI megaproofs rely heavily on explicit forward `have` steps (`79.66`–`158.90` per `1,000` LOC, peaking at `158.90` in `navier-stokes` and `128.56` in `riemann-hypothesis`) and almost never invoke newer search tactics (`aesop` `0.000`–`0.002 / 1k LOC`, `grind` `0.000`–`0.015 / 1k LOC`). Human projects use `gcongr` (`24.47 / 1k LOC` in `carleson`), `norm_num` (`121.51 / 1k LOC` in `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd`, driven by explicit numerical tables), `fun_prop` (`5.63`–`7.31 / 1k LOC` in `SpherePacking` and `carleson`), `aesop` (`0.26`–`1.19 / 1k LOC`), and `grind` (`0.58`–`3.52 / 1k LOC`).
   - **Resource limits and linters:** All `4 / 4` human projects enable `mathlibStandardSet` linters in `lakefile.toml`; `0 / 3` AI megaproofs do. `riemann-hypothesis` sets a **project-wide global `maxHeartbeats = 4000000`** (`20×` Lean's `200,000` default) and `--tstack=400000` in [`lakefile.toml#L4-L10`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/lakefile.toml#L4-L10), **plus `57,556` file-local `set_option maxHeartbeats` overrides** across `54,080` files (`4` set `maxHeartbeats 0` = unlimited, `1` reaches `64,000,000`), `7,381` `synthInstance.maxHeartbeats` overrides, `34` `maxRecDepth` overrides up to `1,000,000`, `38` `set_option linter.all false` overrides, and `151` `decide +kernel` invocations across `124` lines (`34` files). Conversely, `NavierStokesAndEuler` achieves **`0` `set_option` overrides** across all `2,659` files after a dedicated post-synthesis optimization pass that left `13` orphaned `*NoOptions.lean` / `*Investigation.lean` sandboxes on disk. Among human projects, `FLT`, `carleson`, and `SpherePacking` have **`0` `maxHeartbeats` and `0` `maxRecDepth` overrides**, whereas `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` has `27` `maxHeartbeats` (up to `12,000,000`, plus `2` unlimited `0`s in `Unused/`), `366` `maxRecDepth` overrides (up to `100,000`), and `83` `native_decide` calls concentrated in generated explicit-table modules under `IEANTN/`.
3. **Live `sorry` census (`386` active `sorry`s across `180,815` modules; `0` in `riemann-hypothesis`):**
   - Across the three AI megaproofs, **100% of all `28` active `sorry`s** (`0` in `riemann-hypothesis`, `24` in `navier-stokes`, `4` in `NavierStokesAndEuler`) sit in standalone Comparator challenge-specification files and are paired with `0`-`sorry` proofs in `Solution` modules (though `navier-stokes`'s main theorems remain conditional on an unbundled external 128-bit Arb/FLINT certificate).
   - Across the four human projects (`358` active `sorry`s), live inspection shows sharp internal stratification: in `carleson` (`6` total), `3` are Comparator `Challenge.lean` stubs and the only `3` library `sorry`s sit in a single `ToMathlib` Lorentz real-interpolation file (`LorentzInterpolation.lean`); in `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` (`231` total), **both `MediumPNT` (`13`-file cone) and `StrongPNT` (`14`-file cone) have `0` `sorry`s**, `9` sit in auxiliary chapters (`IwaniecKowalskiCh1.lean` `7`, `Wiener.lean` `2`), and `222` (`96.1%`) belong to the separate `IEANTN/` explicit-bounds catalog (`142` `@[blueprint]` literature-survey theorems, `30` trusted-numerics placeholders, `50` explicit-estimate proof obligations); `FLT` (`60` `sorry`s + `3` axioms including `knownin1980s`) and `SpherePacking` (`61` `sorry`s, `49` in `MagicFunction/`) are active multi-year collaborative formalizations with explicit architectural blueprints.
4. **Near-zero symbol reuse between `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` and `riemann-hypothesis` despite shared classical zero-free region mathematics:**
   - Across `8,990` unique qualified names in `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` and `1,054,320` in `riemann-hypothesis`, only **`7` qualified names (`0.08%`)** coincide (all accidental short-name collisions such as `E`, `Psi`, `R`, `W1`, `e`, `gg`, `shift`). Among `1,044` number-theoretic short names in `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` and `67,000` in `riemann-hypothesis`, only **`17` (`1.63%`)** match (`3` genuine elementary prime/zeta lemmas, `1` shared concept `ZeroFactor` encoded with incompatible types, and `13` cross-domain homonyms). Even where `riemann-hypothesis` proves the exact classical Hadamard–de la Vallée Poussin zero-free region (`Formalism/ZeroFreeRegion/`, `34` live modules reaching all five headline theorems), it encodes intermediate lemmas as nullary `: Prop` Horn clauses across `1`-theorem leaf files rather than reusable parameterized functions and Mathlib-ready `lemma`s.

## 1. Cross-Corpus Structural Profile

### 1.1 Measurement Definitions and Denominators

All structural metrics below follow `leanre/scanner.py` and `leanre/stylometry.py` (`leanre.corpus_comparison/1`) on each repository's configured `src_dir`:
- **Library modules (`Modules`) vs. raw `.lean` files:** `discover_lean_files` walks `src_dir` while skipping non-library helper directories (`blueprint*`, `scripts`, `examples`, `.lake`, `build`, and root `lakefile.lean`) unless whitelisted by `lakefile.toml`. Only `9` helper files exist outside the library sets across all seven git trees (`6` `blueprint-verso/` files in `FLT`, `1` `examples/` file in `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd`, `1` `scripts/SideSkimmer/lakefile.lean` in `carleson`, and `1` `lean/scripts/PrintAxioms.lean` in `navier-stokes`); none of those `9` files contain `sorry`.
- **Lines (`LOC`) and `Comments %`:** `LOC` is newline count (`lines_wc`). `Comments %` is `100 × comment_lines / LOC` computed by `stylometry.count_lean_comment_lines_and_heartbeats` (tracking `--` line comments and nested `/- ... -/` blocks outside string literals). `Doc-comment files %` is the share of modules containing `/-!` or `/--`. File-size `p95` uses nearest-rank percentile $\lceil 0.95 N \rceil$.
- **Declarations (`Decls`) and `Thm / Def`:** Top-level declarations matched by `scanner._RE_DECL`, partitioned into `Thms = theorem + lemma`, `Defs = def + abbrev + instance + opaque`, `Inds = structure + class + inductive`, and `Axioms = axiom`. `Thm/Def` is `Thms / Defs`. *(Reconciliation note: in `outputs/leanre.db`, legacy `corpus_stats` bundles `riemann-hypothesis`'s `8,450` `Inds` with `234,220` `Defs` as `242,670` non-theorems; live source scanning separates `234,220` `Defs` and `8,450` `Inds`, yielding `Thm/Def = 3.50` vs. `3.37` against `Defs + Inds`.)*
- **`Private %` and `Noncomputable %`:** Reported both for **explicit per-declaration modifiers** (`private` / `noncomputable` on the declaration header, denominator = `Decls`) and for **effective `noncomputable` definition scope** (share of `Defs` carrying explicit `noncomputable` OR declared inside an active `noncomputable section`). Note also that `FLT` (`262 / 265` files), `carleson` (`127 / 127`), `SpherePacking` (`73 / 73`), and `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd` (`134 / 234`) adopt Lean `v4.35+`'s `module` system (`@[expose] public section`), whereas `riemann-hypothesis` (`890 / 175,187` files, `0.51%`), `navier-stokes` (`50 / 2,270`, `2.20%`), and `NavierStokesAndEuler` (`24 / 2,659`, `0.90%`) rarely use `module`.
- **Identifier length, dot-depth, and `name_compress_ratio`:** Length (in characters) and dot count (`qual_name.count('.')`, where `0` dots = `1` component) are measured over all named top-level declarations (excluding unnamed `instance : ...` headers synthesized as `_anon_<line>`). `name_compress_ratio` matches `stylometry.compute_name_compress_ratio` exactly: `len(zlib.compress("\n".join(sorted(names)).encode("utf-8"), level=6)) / len(raw_bytes)`.

### 1.3 Structural Observations

- **Typeclass instances vs. concrete structures:** `FLT` defines `691` `instance` declarations (`18.52%` of all declarations — algebraic/topological morphisms on adeles, quaternion algebras, and Galois representations), yielding the lowest `Thm/Def` ratio (`1.56`). By contrast, `navier-stokes` defines only `58` `instance`s (`0.15%`) and `riemann-hypothesis` `8,199` (`0.77%`), instead bundling hypotheses into `structure` records (`845` in `navier-stokes`, `8,286` in `riemann-hypothesis`, `667` in `NavierStokesAndEuler`) or nullary `: Prop` `def`s.
- **Explicit vs. section-scoped `noncomputable`:** Looking solely at explicit `noncomputable` header prefixes (`0.01%` in `navier-stokes`, `0.42%` in `carleson`, `0.81%` in `riemann-hypothesis`) would misclassify those libraries as computable. Tracking `noncomputable section` scopes shows that `94.93%`–`99.82%` of all `Defs` in the three AI megaproofs and `77.99%`–`86.97%` in real/harmonic-analysis human projects (`carleson`, `SpherePacking`) are noncomputable, compared to `28.19%` in algebra-heavy `FLT`.
- **Short leaf names inside deep qualified prefixes:** `riemann-hypothesis` and `navier-stokes` combine short local identifiers (median `11.0` and `14.0` characters, e.g. `h1`, `c_zero`, `step1`, `bound_aux`) with deep multi-segment namespaces (median `3.0` and `4.0` dots, mean qualified length `43.89` and `53.03` chars). Human libraries use longer self-describing Mathlib-style short names (`16.0`–`21.0` median chars in `carleson`, `FLT`, `PrimeNumberTheoremAnd`) inside shallower `1`-dot namespaces (median `1.0` dot across all four human projects), explaining why human declaration lists have `25%`–`76%` higher zlib entropy than `navier-stokes` (`0.1126`) and `NavierStokesAndEuler` (`0.1173`).
- **Largest-file outliers:** Each corpus's maximum-length module is verified at [`FLT/Slop/PGL2/FiniteSubgroups/FieldReconstruction.lean#L1`](https://github.com/ImperialCollegeLondon/FLT/blob/019805815d7da565ed1e9f427b6fbd0cbc9d00d3/FLT/Slop/PGL2/FiniteSubgroups/FieldReconstruction.lean#L1) (`1,772` LOC — explicitly placed in `FLT/Slop/`, Kevin Buzzard's quarantine folder for AI-drafted proofs awaiting Mathlib-style cleanup), [`PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/IEANTN/CH2/CH2_part1.lean#L1`](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/blob/a4fb6989b07e9c76b511807ae93e33e25b820e9b/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/IEANTN/CH2/CH2_part1.lean#L1) (`6,478` LOC), [`Carleson/ForestOperator/LargeSeparation.lean#L1`](https://github.com/fpvandoorn/carleson/blob/f7487cc9da4d1e73f009dc0280cd5b958f3e2c78/Carleson/ForestOperator/LargeSeparation.lean#L1) (`2,081` LOC), [`SpherePacking/Basic/PeriodicPacking.lean#L1`](https://github.com/thefundamentaltheor3m/Sphere-Packing-Lean/blob/e07215872319dcadd7ced28670ae4daa13b74b8d/SpherePacking/Basic/PeriodicPacking.lean#L1) (`1,225` LOC), [`src/Formalism/HermitianCurvature/HermitianCurvatureProofs/IsAdmissibleSplittingCharPairMulTwist.lean#L1`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HermitianCurvature/HermitianCurvatureProofs/IsAdmissibleSplittingCharPairMulTwist.lean#L1) (`17,981` LOC), [`lean/CellLayerChallenge.lean#L1`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/CellLayerChallenge.lean#L1) (`7,086` LOC), and [`Euler/EulerProof.lean#L1`](https://github.com/openai/NavierStokesAndEuler/blob/f9e8bc5b38b6e212696e8a30e3e91517af887bbd/Euler/EulerProof.lean#L1) (`20,755` LOC).
