> **Target & Scope:** Statement- and proof-body trace of upstream weighted local bounds and `sEEofTT` at `40e56bab` + `kernel.db` (`dom` `tree_id=0`). ## 0. Findings

1. **The upstream fifth power is the same $2+3$ count, not a second geometry `[L]`.** In [the proof of the weighted bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/PowFourLeRpowNineHalves.lean#L106) exactly one factor is natively quintic: a moment bound costing $b^{(C+12)m^{2}}$ per unit of minor weight, raised to a weight exponent $a\le C_am^{3}$. The cubic bound on $a$ is a parameter that the consumer sets to $C_a=2(11+A_w)$, i.e. twice the cubic count $\sum_lN_Q(l)\le(11+A_w)M^{3}$ of the earlier audit, and the weight is the same leading minor. The two other factors are natively $m^{4}$.
2. **The hypothesis is fully discharged `[K][L]`.** [One theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/WeightedBoundRotatedUniformOfCubicThreshold.lean#L23) (`dom` 1,873) proves it for every $C_b$; its remaining inputs (a cubic Zuckerman threshold and the input proposition) are discharged further up, so [the uniform weighted bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/ThmBFinal.lean#L7) (`dom` 2,368) has no hypotheses. Its backward cone contains no declaration under the `GlobalTheory.Published.` prefix `[K]`.
3. **The zero default is not the branch in force on the live path `[L][K]`.** The producer feeds a stored proof of the guard proposition together with $c>0$ into the only lemmas that evaluate the map; all five `unfold` sites rewrite with the positive branch and no file mentioning the map uses the negative one.
4. **The three implication-shaped fields are conditional in form only `[L][K]`.** At the single construction site each is filled by a function that ignores its guard and returns an unconditionally proved conclusion.
5. **Theta-lift adjoint injectivity enters at Stage One U through the weight-one saturation branch `[K]`.** It is outside the cones of both the bundle-witness theorem and the closing lemma; the three branches meet only at [the Stage One U bundle theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/MemberBundlesLargeUnramifiedExponents.lean#L34) (`dom` 37,317).

### 1.2 The statement `[S]`
Write $m=2r+2$, $n=2r+k+1$. For all $C\ge0$ and $C_a$ there are $C_5\ge0$ and $C_{g0}$ such that for $C_g\ge C_{g0}$, every local datum of rank $(k,r)$, every $K$-finite discrete-series coefficient $f$ with real parameter $\lambda$, and every local point $\gamma$ with $G_p(\gamma)>0$:

$$\lVert\mathrm{mnr}_j(\gamma)\rVert^{2a}\,E_w(f)(\gamma)\;\le\;b^{C_5m^{5}}\,\max\!\big(1,G_p(\gamma)^{-1}\big)^{40(r+1)^{2}}\,J(f),\qquad j\le r,\quad a\le C_am^{3},$$

under: $|\lambda_s|\le b$; a two-block shape ($0\le\lambda_s\le b_v/8$ for $s+1<k$, $-2b_v\le\lambda_s\le-b_v$ otherwise) with $C_gn^{7}\le b_v$; gaps $\lambda_s-\lambda_{s'}\ge C_gm^{4}$ for $s<s'$; a gap condition at scale $C_gY$ with [the gap scale](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/PowFourLeRpowNineHalves.lean#L13) $Y=m^{9/2}\big(3(r+1)n^{2}+n^{3}\big)$; $1\le T,T'\le b^{Cm}$; and one of two type cases (types differing by $\ge30(r+1)^{2}+6$ at one index plus a domain condition, or equal types with one gap $\ge2(30(r+1)^{2}+6)+1$). Here [the weight](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/ProductMoment.lean#L18) is the leading $(j+1)\times(j+1)$ minor of the point matrix. The readings of $E_w$ (pointwise orbital energy), $J$ (norm square) and $G_p$ are `[M]`: those definitions were not unfolded.

### 1.3 Where $m^{5}$ and $m^{3}$ arise `[L]`
Put $e=(r+1)a\le|C_a|m^{4}$. The proof composes three inputs.

- **Weight to shifts** ([the weighted reduction](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/CoefOfOfWsp.lean#L224), statement only `[S]`). If $E_w(F)(\gamma)\le K(\gamma)J(F)$ for all $F$ in the coefficient spaces of the shifted parameters $\lambda+\nu$, $\nu\in\mathbb Z^{n}$, $\lVert\nu\rVert_1\le e$, and the gap exceeds $t(n,\varepsilon)+1+2e$, then $\lVert\mathrm{mnr}_j\rVert^{2a}E_w(f)\le(2n+1)^{2e}K\cdot\mathrm{mom}_{j,a}(f)$, where [the moment](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/ProductMoment.lean#L435) integrates the left side over big-cell points.
- **Unweighted endpoint bound** at each shifted parameter, through [a monotonicity lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/PowFourLeRpowNineHalves.lean#L58): $K(\gamma)=b^{\max(C_1,C_2,0)m^{4}}\max(1,1/G_p)^{40(r+1)^{2}}$, valid because the shifted gap is still $\ge C_gY-2e$.
- **Moment bound** (second conjunct of the input proposition): $\mathrm{mom}_{j,a}(f)\le b^{(C+12)m^{2}a}J(f)$.

$$\underbrace{(2n+1)^{2e}}_{\le\,b^{4e}\,\le\,b^{4|C_a|m^{4}}}\cdot\underbrace{b^{\max(C_1,C_2,0)m^{4}}}_{\text{endpoint}}\cdot\underbrace{b^{(C+12)m^{2}a}}_{\le\,b^{(C+12)|C_a|m^{5}}}\;\le\;b^{C_5m^{5}},\qquad C_5=4|C_a|+\max(C_1,C_2,0)+(C+12)|C_a|.$$

So `[M]` $5=2+3$: quadratic cost of one unit of minor weight times the cubic range of the weight exponent; the shift count and the endpoint bound are quartic and rounded up. The cubic range also explains the quartic gap hypothesis: the shift radius is $e\le|C_a|m^{4}$, and the proof needs $100(q_0+1)\le C_gm^{4}$ for $q_0=e+40(r+1)^{3}+(r+1)(r+2)$, which holds once $C_{g0}\ge10^{6}+100|C_a|$. The separate cubic $t(n,\varepsilon)\le C_tn^{3}$ is a threshold in $n$, absorbed by $C_gY$; it is not the source of $a\le C_am^{3}$.

### 1.4 Transfer and consumer `[L]`
[The transfer lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/WeightedUniformRotatedOfExpansion.lean#L30) (`dom` 129) instantiates the bound at $C=2|C_h|$ with the same $C_a$, identifies the weight with the leading minor of the frame point and $G_p$ with the frame quantity, and multiplies by two factors $\pi^{2m^{2}}\le b^{4m^{5}}$, giving the constant $C_5+8$ in [the local statement](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/CompanionTheory/AutomorphicTheory/BesselWaveGrids/WeightedUniformRotatedOfExpansion.lean#L20). (the last step is [the cubic count](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/CuspSpanNormBounds/TheoremBConstants.lean#L957)).
