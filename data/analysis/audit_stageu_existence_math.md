# `StageU` Existence Subtree and the `k/n² ≤ x^{1/2-ε}` Family Bound: Mathematical Audit

> **Target & Scope:** Statement- and proof-body trace of the `StageU` existence subtree at `40e56bab` + `kernel.db` (`dom` `tree_id=0`). Tags: `[K]` kernel graph, `[S]` statement/def text, `[L]` proof-body lines read, `[M]` mathematical reconstruction; anything untagged `[L]` has an `UNREAD` proof body (§6; see [Scope & Tag Legend](README.md)).
> **Question.** The exposition's reading guide flags the family scales $m\asymp x^{1/2}$, $n\asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$, $k\asymp x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}$ and the size row $k\le n^2x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$ as coming from the existence subtree below `GlobalTheory.T.large_unramified_exponent_bundles_exist_alt` (`37,318` decls). This audit traces where those exponents are fixed, what that subtree proves, and whether existence could be vacuous.

## 0. Findings

1. **The exponents are not decided inside the subtree `[K][S]`.** They are fixed by one definition, [RankVal](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/GlobalTwisting/PairRowIffQdRows.lean#L28), and realized by a 9-decl arithmetic lemma, [exists_ranks_rankVal_frame](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/ExistsRanksRankValFrame.lean#L45), whose dominator chain runs through the *consumer* (`UpperTheory.Top.supplyChi`, `2,686` decls); a directed-path query from the subtree root to that lemma returns no path. The subtree only *reads* the ranks, as a field of every scale context it quantifies over.
2. **Exact algebra `[L]`.** $k/n^2\le 2x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}/x^{4+16\varepsilon}=2x^{1/2-6\varepsilon}$, so the size row $k\le n^2x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$ holds with a spare factor $x^{5\varepsilon}/2$. The row is not the binding constraint for the exponent $\tfrac12$ (§1.3).
3. **Origin of $2$ and $\tfrac92$ `[M]` from `[S]`.** A lower row $m^5\cdot f\cdot x^{2\varepsilon}\lesssim k$ and the upper row $k\le x^{1/2}n\cdot nx^{-\varepsilon}$ sandwich $k$; with $m\asymp x^{1/2}$ they force $n\gtrsim x^{2}$ and $k\gtrsim x^{9/2}$. The chosen $k$ sits $\approx0.29\,\varepsilon$ above the lower row in the exponent (§1.2).
4. **What the subtree proves `[S]`.** For every hypothetical scale context $K$ (carrying its own ranks, host field and weights) beyond a threshold: membership of a four-stage construction ([MemberE](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Definitions/AutomorphicMembership/ThetaChr.lean#L102)) and existence of a seven-field bundle ([BundleCondition](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/ThetaCorrespondence/ThetaPairLifts/ThirdPlaceSeries/ColumnChoiceWithTies.lean#L47)) at every base point. Its inputs are a 60-field record of published automorphic and Galois-theoretic statements; the geometry slot is instantiated at the always-true predicate.
5. **Existence is genuine but factored `[S][L]`.** The $\exists$ over families is assembled in three places (consumer host supply, a producer-side conditional, a consumer-side closing lemma), and non-emptiness on unbounded windows is a structural field of the family record. No degenerate rank or empty-index escape was found; two tool blind spots that initially suggested one are recorded in §5.

**Why $2$ and $\tfrac92$.** Conjunct 1 of the same proposition is a lower row, and its proof bookkeeping is explicit `[L]`:

$$x^{\frac{\varepsilon}{100}}\,m^{5}\,f\,L\,x^{\varepsilon}\ \le\ x^{\frac92+9.7135\,\varepsilon}\ \le\ x^{\frac92+10\varepsilon}\ \le\ k .$$

So $\log_xk$ must lie in $[\tfrac92+9.71\varepsilon,\ \tfrac92+15\varepsilon]$; the realized value leaves $\approx0.29\,\varepsilon$ below and $5\varepsilon$ above. Eliminating $k$ between the two rows gives $n\gtrsim m^{5}x^{3\varepsilon-1/2}\asymp x^{2+2.7\varepsilon}$ `[M]`. Hence, once $m\asymp x^{1/2}$, the integers are forced by the fifth power of $m$: $2=\tfrac52-\tfrac12$ and $\tfrac92=\tfrac52+2$ `[M]`. Which analytic step needs $m^{5}$ (the record field is `k_low`, with a named exponent `jG`) is `UNREAD`.

### 1.3 Consequence for the exponent $\tfrac12$ `[M]`

In the error bracket of the exposition's §3.4, with $n_{T2}\le n$ and $n_T\ge\tfrac12nx^{-\varepsilon/8}$,

$$\frac{n_{T2}}{n_T}\cdot\frac{k}{n^{2}}\,x^{2\varepsilon}\ \le\ 4\,x^{\frac12-\frac{31}{8}\varepsilon},\qquad\text{while}\qquad \frac{a_E}{mb}\le2x^{\frac12+\frac{\varepsilon}{16}},\quad 2m\,\frac{n}{n_T}\,x^{\varepsilon}\le8\,x^{\frac12+\frac{17}{16}\varepsilon}.$$

The $k/n^2$ term is smaller than the other two by a power of $x$. The exponent $\tfrac12$ is decided by balancing $x/m$ against $m$; the row $k\le n^2x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$ is a comfortable side condition, satisfied with $x^{5\varepsilon}$ to spare.
