## 0. Thesis and Architecture

For a character $\chi'$ let $G_{\chi'}$ be the first Riesz mean of $\psi(y,\chi')$ with its polar part removed `[L]`:

$$G_{\chi'}(y)=\sum_{n\le y}\Lambda(n)\chi'(n)\Bigl(\frac{y}{n}-1\Bigr)+y\,\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi')\quad(\chi'\neq 1),\qquad G_{1}(y)=\sum_{n\le y}\Lambda(n)\Bigl(\frac{y}{n}-1\Bigr)-y(\log y-1-\gamma).$$

**Thesis `[M]`.** Fix a primitive $\chi$ of order $o$. The formal proof establishes one inequality: on arbitrarily long windows $y\in[Y,Y^{3/2}]$,

$$\mathrm{Gm}(y):=G_1(y)+\frac{1}{3o+1}\sum_{1\neq\chi'\in\langle\chi\rangle}\operatorname{Re}G_{\chi'}(y)\ \le\ y^{\frac12+9\varepsilon}\qquad(0<\varepsilon<\tfrac1{52}).$$

A windowed form of Landau's theorem converts this one-sided bound into $L(s,\chi^j)\neq 0$ for $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac12+9\varepsilon$, and $\varepsilon\to0$. The bound itself is of Arakelov type: by Fourier analysis on a finite abelian Galois group, $\mathrm{Gm}(x)$ is embedded in a Frobenius-weighted prime-power sum $\mathrm{ASK}$, and eleven real inequalities between slopes and heights of a family of rank-$m$ Hermitian lattices (built from unitary theta lifts) force $\mathrm{ASK}\le\mathrm{MAIN}+\text{error}$ with relative error $\frac1m+\frac mx+\frac{k}{n^2x}$. Taking $m\asymp x^{1/2}$ yields the exponent $\frac12$. Characters outside $\langle\chi\rangle$ that the Fourier analysis introduces are quadratic twists by two auxiliary primes, chosen by a classical mean-square argument so that their $G$ is $\le x^{1/2+\varepsilon}$.

## 1. Step 1 — Reduction to a One-Sided Windowed Bound

1. **Target `[L]`.** [EveryCharacter](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/EveryCharacter.lean#L13) states $L(s,\chi)\neq0$ for all $N\ge1$, $\chi$ mod $N$, $\operatorname{Re}s>\frac12$, $(\chi\neq1\lor s\neq1)$. [riemannHypothesis](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Main.lean#L4) and [LFunction_eq_zero_re](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Main.lean#L10) follow by the functional equation and nonvanishing Euler factors.
2. **Mellin side `[L]`.** For $\operatorname{Re}s>2$ and $\chi'\neq1$, $\int_1^\infty G_{\chi'}(y)\,y^{-s-1}dy=-\dfrac{\frac{L'}{L}(s,\chi')-s\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi')}{s(s-1)}$, with the analogous identity for $\zeta$. Hence $\Phi(s)=\int_0^\infty\mathrm{Gm}(e^u)e^{-su}du$ ([Level.Gm](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/ExplicitFormulaErrorOfHostConditions.lean#L38)) continues meromorphically, and after symmetrizing $\chi',\overline{\chi'}$ every zero $\rho$ with $0<\operatorname{Re}\rho<1$ of any $L(s,\chi')$ is a simple pole with residue $S/(-\rho(\rho-1))$, $S>0$ ([poleData](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/poleData.lean#L248)).
3. **Windowed Landau `[L]`.** Assume $\mathrm{Gm}(y)\le y^\theta$ on $[Y,Y^{3/2}]$ for arbitrarily large $Y$; put $g(u)=\mathrm{Gm}(e^u)$, $U=\log Y$. Since $|g|+g=2\max(g,0)\le2e^{\theta u}$ on $[U,\frac32U]$, [landau_window_ineq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindowIneq.lean#L27) gives for $\sigma>2$, $k\in\mathbb N$, $\eta>0$:

   $$\Bigl|\frac{\Phi^{(k)}(\sigma+i\gamma)}{k!}\Bigr|+\operatorname{Re}\frac{(-1)^k\Phi^{(k)}(\sigma)}{k!}\ \le\ \frac{2e^{\eta U}}{(\sigma-\theta)^k}+2C_0\int_{(0,\infty)\setminus[U,\frac32U]}\frac{u^ke^{(2-\sigma)u}}{k!}\,du .$$

   With $k=\lceil\frac54\sigma U\rceil$ the kernel $u^ke^{-\sigma u}$ concentrates inside the window and the tail is $O(\sigma^{-k})$ ([landau_tails](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauTails.lean#L80)). A pole at distance $d<\sigma-\theta$ from $\sigma+i\gamma$ forces the left side to be $\ge c\,d^{-k}-Me^{-k}$ ([landau_nearest](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/LandauNearest.lean#L36)), a contradiction as $U\to\infty$. There is no Perron contour and no power-sum method; positivity enters only through $|g|+g\ge0$ and $S>0$.
4. **Parameters `[L]`.** Given a hypothetical zero $s_0$, $\theta=\frac12+\frac12\min(\operatorname{Re}s_0-\frac12,\frac12)$ and [level_of_tiedWin](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/RamificationFrobenius/TotientGaloisClasses/TopWin.lean#L68) shrinks $\varepsilon$ until $9\varepsilon\le\theta-\frac12$; [core_gm](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/CoreGm.lean#L12) then supplies $\mathrm{Gm}(x)\le x^{1/2+(e_D+e_L+7)\varepsilon}$ with $(e_D,e_L)=(2,0)$, and $\varepsilon<\frac1{52}$ keeps $\frac12+9\varepsilon<\frac{35}{52}<1$. Every limit is $x\to\infty$ at fixed $\chi$; no estimate is required to be uniform in the conductor of $\chi$.

## 2. Step 2 — Auxiliary Twists by Mean Square over Prime Moduli

**Statement `[L]`.** For $S(Z,y,q,\kappa)=\sum_{n\le Z}\Lambda(n)\min(1,\frac yn)\kappa(n)\bigl(\frac nq\bigr)$, [meanSqK](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/meanSqK.lean#L51) proves, for every $\|\kappa\|_\infty\le1$, $y\in[Y,Y^K]$, $Z=Y^{400K}$,

$$\sum_{q\in P}|S(Z,y,q,\kappa)|^2\le C\,y\log y\,|P|,\qquad P=\{q\ \text{prime}:\ U^{1/2}<q\le U,\ q\equiv a_0\ (m_0)\},\quad 8\mid m_0,$$

where [admitted](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/PrimeSetTwoBounds.lean#L18) requires $Y^{\varepsilon/6}\le\log U\le Y^{\varepsilon/5}\log^2Y$. The moduli are therefore enormous relative to the sums: $y\le(\log q)^{O(1/\varepsilon)}$.

**Proof `[L]`.** [meanSq_abs](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/MeanSqAbs.lean#L8) expands the square, and quadratic reciprocity (valid since $q>Z^2\ge nn'$ and $q$ is fixed mod $8$) turns $\bigl|\sum_{q\in P}(\frac nq)(\frac{n'}q)\bigr|$ into $\bigl|\sum_{q\in P}(\frac qs)\bigr|$ with $s$ the odd squarefree part of $nn'$ prime to $m_0$. [meansq_split](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/JacobiCharacterSums/MSqMainB.lean#L9) separates three classes:

- $s=1$: the diagonal, $\le C_3\,y\log y+\mathrm{RR}^2$;
- $s=\max(n^\star,2)$: bounded trivially by $|P|$, times the weight of pairs $(n,n')$ of prime powers with squarefree part $n^\star$, which is $\le2B^2+2B\cdot\mathrm{RR}$ with $B=3\log y$ ([pairs_r](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/GL3MeanSquares/GenusMeanAsymptotics/JacobiCharacterSums/MSqPairs.lean#L307));
- all other $s\le Z^2$: [twistK](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/MeanSquareBounds/twistK.lean#L148) gives $\bigl|\sum_{q\in P}(\frac qs)\bigr|\le Y^{-(K+1)}|P|$ from the classical zero-free region and PNT in progressions.

## 3. Step 3 — The Engine: Slope Inequalities Bound a Prime Sum

**3.1 The abelian identity `[L]`.** Let $G=E_0\times H$ be the finite abelian group carrying Frobenius elements $\mathrm{Frob}_p$ of the host field (`[M]`: an abelian CM field containing $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-q})$, $\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-d})$, the field of $\chi$ and a $2$-power cyclotomic layer; $n=|H|\asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$), and $\Phi\subset G$ a distinguished subset of size $f$. For an amplitude $a$, a base point $t$ and positive thresholds $w:G\to\mathbb R$, with $g^\ast$ the partner of $g$ under complex conjugation and $t$, the source defines [ASK](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L84), [MAIN](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L89) and [De](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L93):

$$\mathrm{ASK}=\sum_{p\ \mathrm{inert}}2\log p\sum_{\substack{i\ge1\\ i\,\mathrm{Frob}_p\notin\Phi}}\max\Bigl(\frac a{p^i}-w\bigl((i\,\mathrm{Frob}_p)^\ast\bigr),0\Bigr)+(\text{ramified prime}),\qquad \mathrm{MAIN}=a\Bigl(\log a-1-\gamma+\ell(\chi_C)-\frac1f\sum_{\rho\in\Phi}\log w(\rho)\Bigr),$$

$$\mathrm{De}=\frac1f\sum_{g\notin\Phi}\sum_{\psi\in\widehat G}\overline{\psi(g)}\,w(g^\ast)\,G_\psi\bigl(a/w(g^\ast)\bigr),\qquad \ell(\psi)=\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_\psi).$$

[ch_balance](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChBalance.lean#L200) proves the exact identity $\mathrm{ASK}-\mathrm{MAIN}=\mathrm{De}$ by orthogonality on $G$. On thick classes the thresholds satisfy $w\in[b,b+\eta+1]$ with $b=e^{x^\varepsilon}$ and $|a/b-x|\le1$, so $\mathrm{De}$ samples every $G_\psi$ at height $\approx x$ although the primes in $\mathrm{ASK}$ run up to $a\approx xb$.

**4.2 Relation to known barriers `[M]`.**

1. *Strength of the claimed inequality.* §3.2 is a one-sided upper bound, with the exact leading constant and relative error $x^{-1/2+O(\varepsilon)}$, for prime-power sums restricted to Frobenius classes. Sieve methods cannot produce this (Brun–Titchmarsh loses a factor $2$, and the parity obstruction blocks the sharp constant). The formal proof uses no sieve; the bound is derived from positivity of heights. That does not evade the difficulty, it relocates it: `LinkG` (prime class sums dominated by local gains) together with exact main-term matching in (D) and `Count5` carries essentially the full strength of the conclusion, and those are the least-read parts of the audit.
2. *Square-root barrier.* The first $\frac12$ (Step 2) is elementary. The second is the optimum of $\frac xm+m$, and it exists only because the slope errors in (D), `Slopes` and `HComp` are $c\,n_Ta\,L_a$ with $c$ independent of the rank $m$ and of $x$, and because the near-orthogonality defects are exponentially small. In slope arguments errors normally grow with the rank; a referee should confirm from the proofs, not the statements, that $m$-uniformity is real.
3. *Siegel zeros.* See §2: one exceptional twist is absorbed trivially, bad auxiliary primes are discarded, and the target $\chi$ is fixed while $x\to\infty$, so no conductor-uniform bound is needed. Siegel's theorem appears in the kernel cone but, in the text read, only where an effective bound would do.
4. *Scale separation.* The $L$-functions of $\langle\chi\rangle$ are probed at height $x$ through a host field whose auxiliary primes have $\log q\approx x^{\varepsilon/5}$ and through sums over primes up to $xe^{x^\varepsilon}$. All constants in the `Link` predicates are quantified as $\exists C\,\exists x_0\,\forall x\ge x_0$ `[L]`; uniformity of those constants across the family index is asserted by the statements and proved inside `UNREAD` subtrees.
5. *Non-vacuity.* `ChainTied` is an existential statement. The size predicates force $m\ge x^{1/2-\varepsilon}$, $n_T\ge1$, $n_{T2}\ge nx^{-\varepsilon}$ and positive thresholds `[L]`, and the identification of $\ell(\psi)$ with $\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_\psi)$ is part of the tie `[L]`, but that a family satisfying all eleven conjuncts simultaneously exists for every $x$ in a window is proved only inside the 37,318-declaration existence subtree.
6. *Known slack in the formal text `[L]`.* The bridge from `ChainTied` to `core_gm` discards the bundled pointwise character bounds and uses the Step 2 bounds instead; two slots of the top shape are instantiated to `True`; several literature bundles carry unread fields (see the companion dossiers). None of these weakens a statement used above.

**4.3 Suggested reading order for a skeptic.** (i) `each_unit_bound_statement`: check the cancellation of §3.2 by hand; it is short and decides whether the eleven inequalities really imply the bound. (ii) `LinkG_at` and its proof in `linkG_concrete5`: identify the geometric object whose local lengths dominate $\sum\Lambda$ over a Frobenius class. (iii) `Count5` and the class-count identity behind it: the main terms must match to relative precision $x^{-1/2}$. (iv) `LinkS`: the source of $\exp(-x^{\varepsilon/8})$. (v) The existence subtree, for the simultaneous scales $m\asymp x^{1/2}$, $n\asymp x^{2+8\varepsilon}$, $k\asymp x^{9/2+10\varepsilon}$. (vi) The classical leaves of Step 2, which should be routine.
