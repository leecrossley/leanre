## 4. Verification Limits & Methodological Caveats `[K]`/`[S]`

1. **1-Hop $E_V$ Granularity vs Helper Factoring:** Low 1-hop Jaccard $J(A, B)$ separates monolithic inline tactic proofs from short proofs that factor work into a domain-specific helper (e.g. pairs `#2`, `#3`, `#5`, `#6`, `#7`, `#8`), even when 2-hop cones partially reconverge on foundational `Nat`/`Eq`/`Field` algebra (`J_2 \approx 0.14\text{--}0.33`) `[K]`/`[M]`.
2. **Exact Kernel Signature Scope (`k1n`):** `alien_twins` groups theorems with identical `k1n` kernel types; mathematically equivalent theorems stated over distinct isomorphic custom structures (`s1a` or defeq wrappers) sit in separate `k1n` buckets and are not merged here `[S]`.
3. **Bucket Capping & Opacity:** Huge auxiliary collision buckets (`<= 5,129` `_simp_`/`_proof_` copies) are deterministically capped (`<= 16` named / `<= 4` aux representatives per bucket) for `< 15s` runtime; inner tactic scripts are **`OPAQUE`** (`UNREAD`) and Layer 1 kernel soundness does not establish Layer 4 mathematical peer review `[L]`.
