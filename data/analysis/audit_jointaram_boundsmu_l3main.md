> **Target:** `JointARam` (`dom = 208`), `BoundsMu` / `slope_bounds` (`dom = 79`/`78`), and `L3main` / `FlatDegree` (`dom = 989`), following [the `Places`/`DIV`/balance audit](./audit_places_div_and_balance.md) §3–§4 (`40e56bab`, `dom_tree = ALL`)

## 1. Verdicts

1. **`JointARam` is a proved *frame* slope inequality: Bost + Harder–Narasimhan + one-place linear algebra + layer cake `[L]`.** It bounds the *mean* slope of the target lattice from below, which is stronger than Bost's $\mu_{\min}\le\mu_{\max}+h$. No circularity: the cone has `575` project decls, all in lattice/HN/matrix modules, `0` L-function modules `[K]`. Two corrections to the earlier note: the frame error is $(5\sigma+3\sqrt{de}+4\sqrt{2e_0})\,HH$, and $\log|I|$ is **pure slack**, not an entropy price. A sharp form without it is proved and left unread.
2. **`BoundsMu` is product formula + determinant Cauchy–Schwarz, not periods `[L]`.** Positive-definiteness of the $K_0$-rational form $H_m$ enters only through [DetCS](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/ProdFormula.lean#L17): $|\det(AHB^\ast)|^2\le\det(AHA^\ast)\det(BHB^\ast)$. No Shimura period, Faltings height or CM abelian variety is used.
3. **`L3main` is bookkeeping. $\log\Omega$ is a *definition*, and the real content is the `covol` row `[L][K]`.** [levelThree_bound_closed](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/Divisibility/EigenvalueSumLeClosedOf.lean#L94) (`dom` `2`) is an equality by unfolding. The mathematics sits in [IndefData.L3](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/IndefiniteForms/IndefSide.lean#L315), i.e. $\mu(M_j)\ge-l_{3,j}$. That row is discharged via `CovolumeBoundOfX`, whose main term genuinely descends from a **proved Chowla–Selberg + Kronecker-limit** chain (§4).
4. **Next unread engine: `UAt`**, the uniform lower bound $-C\,\mathrm{unitE}\le\mu(\text{weight-}0\text{ lattice})$ from [atPoint](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DivisorProofs/UniformLowerBoundOfSums.lean#L39) (height averaging + arithmetic Hilbert–Samuel). It is the only place where the lower bound on $\mu(M_j)$ gets its strength (§6).

## 2. `JointARam` Unpacked `[L]`

**Statement.** [JointARam](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FrameTraceRatioFinEquiv.lean#L197), over a totally complex $K$, with maps $B_i:V_i\to W$ ($i\in I$), $d_i=\operatorname{rk}N_i$ and $cc\subset W$:
$$s-\log|I|-\frac{4u\log(1+HH/u)+(5\sigma+3\sqrt{de}+4\sqrt{2e_0})\,HH}{1-\delta}\ \le\ \mu(cc),\qquad \mu=\texttt{muDef}=2\,\mathrm{slope}-\log2+\tfrac{\log|d_K|}{[K:\mathbb Q]}.$$
Its hypotheses are:

- [LatGeom](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FrameTraceRatioFinEquiv.lean#L59): integrality $B_i(N_i)\subseteq cc$, heights $\mathrm{htMap}(B_i)\le h_i$ (mean over places of $\log\|B_i\|_v^2$), $\mathrm{Ball}_i=Y'^{-1}[B_i]_vY_i$ at one complex place $v$, and padding $(1-\delta)|\kappa\sqcup\kappa_1|\le|\kappa|$.
- [LatNum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FrameTraceRatioFinEquiv.lean#L79): $s\sum d_i\le\sum d_i(\mu(N_i)-h_i)$, $\mu_{\max}(N_i)-h_i\le s+u$, and $s-\mu_{\min}(cc)\le HH$.
- [InputSG](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FrameTraceRatioFinEquiv.lean#L30): $\|K_{ij}\|_{HS}^2\le(1+\eta_0)d_id_j/D^\ast$ for $i\ne j$, $\|K_{ii}-1\|_{HS}^2\le e_0d_i$, and $|\alpha|\le(1+\eta_1)D^\ast$, together with $\mathrm{Ball}_i=B^f_iV^r_i$, $V_i^{r\ast}V^r_i=1$, $\sigma\le1/20$.

**Proof chain.** `joint_a_ramified_holds` (`dom` `208`) runs through the following steps.

- It takes conjunct `.1` of [ramification_condition_holds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/JointDModelAppliesUnitLattice.lean#L215).
- [ramification_linear_case_holds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/JointDModelAppliesUnitLattice.lean#L164) does matrix algebra (`compressTraceSquareBound`, `compressDiagDefectBound`) to turn `InputSG` into a frame trace ratio $\eta=5\sigma+3\sqrt{de}$ and a frame deviation $e=2e_0$.
- `flat_lattice_theorem_holds` then calls `theoremF`. That is [theoremF_of_sharp](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/TheoremFSharp.lean#L390) applied to `frame_theorem_sharp`, which is [theoremFSharp_of](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/TheoremFSharp.lean#L168) applied to [frame_linear_algebra_first](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/FrameLinearAlgebraFirst.lean#L77).

`theoremFSharp_of` has four steps, one for each threshold $\tau\in[0,HH]$.

- **(a) Markov.** [thmF_share](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/TheoremFSharp.lean#L11) gives $\sum_i(d_i-\operatorname{rk}N_i^{\tau})\le\frac{u}{u+\tau}\sum_id_i$. Here $N_i^\tau$ is the HN step of $N_i$ with normalized slopes $-h_i\ge s-\tau$. It uses only the mean and maximum bounds of `LatNum`.
- **(b) Bost + HN maximality.** [slope_inequality_mean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/SlopeIneqMean.lean#L249) gives $\mu_{\min}(B_iA)\ge\mu_{\min}(A)-\mathrm{htMap}(B_i)$. Then `frameB_hnStep` gives $B_i(N_i^\tau)\subseteq S^\tau$, the HN step of $cc$ at level $s-\tau$. The single-map engine is [HN.SlopeIneq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/Statement.lean#L91): if $\|\varphi x\|\le C\|x\|$ then $\mu_{\min}(L)-\log C\le\mu_{\min}(\operatorname{im}\varphi)$. It is proved by [slope_inequality_general](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/SlopeIneq.lean#L507) from slope and saturation characterizations, with archimedean positivity supplied by the Hermitian norms `realize b X`.
- **(c) One complex place.** [FrameLA1](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/StatementFrameF.lean#L11) states $\dim\bigl(\sum_iB_iN_i^\tau\bigr)^\perp\le(\eta+4\varepsilon+4\sqrt e)\dim H$, where $\varepsilon=\frac u{u+\tau}$. Its proof uses Cauchy–Schwarz on $\sum\sqrt{d_i}\,\|cB_i^\ast B_i-1\|_{HS}$ and trace bounds for $Q=\sum c_iB_iB_i^\ast$. [thmF_place](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/HarderNarasimhanProofs/TheoremFSharp.lean#L35) projects $\kappa\sqcup\kappa_1\to\kappa$, giving $|\kappa|-\operatorname{rk}S^\tau\le w(\tau)|\kappa|$ with $w(\tau)=\frac{\eta+4u/(u+\tau)+4\sqrt e}{1-\delta}$.
- **(d) Layer cake.** All slopes of $cc$ are at least $s-HH$, so $\mu(cc)\ge s-\int_0^{HH}\min(1,w)\,d\tau$, and $\int_0^{HH}w=\frac{4u\log(1+HH/u)+(\eta+4\sqrt e)HH}{1-\delta}$.

**Slack, positivity, circularity `[L][K][M]`.**

- `theoremF_of_sharp` only subtracts $\log|I|\ge0$ (from `Nonempty I`). Conjunct `.2` of `ramification_condition_holds` is the same bound *without* $\log|I|$, and `hygiene` flags it as `tuple_unread`. The earlier reading "price of $|W|$ maps" in [the Places audit](./audit_places_div_and_balance.md) is therefore wrong `[M]`.
- Positivity comes from Hermitian norms, Bost's inequality and HN maximality, plus the near-tight frame at one place. There is no Minkowski or Hodge-index input and no `le_refl` slot (`hygiene --semantic`: `0` semantic findings).
- `[S]` This is a mean-slope version of the Bost/Chen frame argument. Lower-bounding $\mu(cc)$, not $\mu_{\max}$, is what lets row (S) cap $\mathrm{wmean}(\mu(N_u)-h)$.

## 3. `BoundsMu` / `slope_bounds` Unpacked `[L]`

**Statement** ([BoundsMu](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/DivisorInequality/StatementObj.lean#L17), proved by [slope_bounds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/DivisorInequality/DivisorInequalityProofs/BaseLevelBoundsWithCovolumeHolds.lean#L47); `boundsMu` is an alias, `dom` `79`/`78`):
$$\mu(R^\vee)\ \le\ \overline{\log\kappa}+\sum_j\frac{\mathrm{lcv}(M_j)}{D}+2\sum_{j,\,y\in T_w}\ell_yF_{y,j}+LG+\sum_j(E_{1,j}+E_{2,j})+E_3,\qquad \frac{\mathrm{lcv}(M_j)}{D}=-\mu(M_j).$$
So $\mu(R^\vee)+\sum_j\mu(M_j)$ is bounded **above** by the mean $\log\kappa$ plus local index terms. Its hypotheses are:

- a CM subfield $K_0$;
- the determinant factorization $\mathrm{detRatio}(X_Q)=\kappa^D\prod_j\mathrm{detRatio}_j$, at every place for $\mu_0$ and at $v_0$ (twisted by $\rho$) otherwise;
- $H_m$ positive definite at every infinite place with $K_0$-entries, and a quotient metric $X_Q^{-\ast}X_Q^{-1}=(P H_m^{-1}P^\ast)^{-1}$;
- $\Lambda_{\mu_0}=P\cdot M_t$, $R$ locally equal to $\iota(\Lambda)$, and per-prime index bounds.

**Proof chain.**

- `slope_bounds` uses `untwisted_choice` (finite bookkeeping) and [divisor_bounds_chain_weak](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/HermitianTwistDivisors/ConjugateWeightLatticeOfGeneral.lean#L518).
- That calls [second_step_refined_weak](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/HermitianTwistDivisors/ConjugateWeightLatticeOfGeneral.lean#L506).
- **Base step.** [LemmaRb](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/Statement.lean#L120), proved by [reduction_lemma_second](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/HermitianTwistDivisors/ReductionLemmaSecond.lean#L288), gives $\mathrm{lcv}(X_Q,PM_t)\le\sum_w\ell_w\,v_w\bigl(\mathrm{gramR}(\mathrm{quotGram}\,P\,H_m)(e_w)\bigr)$. Its inputs are `prod_formula'`, `determinant_cauchy_schwarz`, `covolume_via_basis`, `index_change_formula` and `gram_matrix_change`.
- **Twist step.** [weak_form_b](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/QuotientMetricDivisors/HermitianTwistDivisors/ConjugateWeightLatticeOfGeneral.lean#L500) transports this through `detRatio` and the local indices. It uses the product formula, index change and conjugate-weight Gram identities.

**Where positivity comes from.** The bound is not computed through Shimura/CM periods. The Gram determinant $G\in K$ satisfies the product formula, which trades $\sum_{v\mid\infty}$ for $-\sum_w\ell_wv_w(G)$. Determinant Cauchy–Schwarz for the positive-definite $H_m$ makes the covolume comparison one-sided. In use, [IndefSide.boundsMu](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/IndefiniteForms/IndefUse.lean#L271) (`dom` `19`) sets $\rho\equiv1$ and replaces $\sum_j\mathrm{lcv}(M_j)/D$ by $\sum_jl_{3,j}$ through the field `S.l3` (row L3). **So row (D) becomes an upper bound exactly when $\mu(M_j)$ is bounded below** `[M]`.
