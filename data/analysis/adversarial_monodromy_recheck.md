## 1. Verdict

**Not broken.** No vacuity, dropped hypothesis, weakened conclusion, or degenerate period ring was found. [deRham_potentiallySemistable](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/InertiaEigenvalues/DeRhamPotentiallySemistable.lean#L11) does state, and kernel-check, the full theorem: every continuous de Rham $\mathbb{Q}_l$-representation of $G_K$, for every finite $K/\mathbb{Q}_l$ and every prime $l$ (including $l=2$), is potentially semistable `[L]`. Kernel cone: `98,269` declarations in total, `14,004` of them project declarations. It uses only `propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`. It has `0` `sorryAx`, `0` `ofReduceBool`/`ofReduceNat`, `0` project `opaque` and `0` `decide_kernel`. There are `93` `of_decide` uses, which the kernel re-checks, so they are harmless `[L]`.

**Downgraded, though: there is no "new textbook-worthy elementary proof" here** `[M]`/`[S]`. The induction (twist the total weight to $0$; use Sen at weight $0$; reduce reducible representations with Hyodo $H^1_g=H^1_{\mathrm{st}}$; apply an elementary weight modification $w\mapsto w+\varepsilon_{x,y}$ to irreducible ones; use $\sum w^2$ as the descent measure) follows the route Colmez published without $p$-adic differential equations (*Espaces vectoriels de dimension finie et représentations de de Rham*, Astérisque 319, 2008). The Fargues–Fontaine curve gives a second Robba-free proof. "Robba-free" is accurate but not new. What is new is engineering: Colmez's Banach–Colmez dimension theory is replaced by explicit Moore determinants plus one Gauss-norm lemma on $A_{\mathrm{cris}}$ (§4).

**Corrections to the earlier audit** `[M]`:
- Its caveat 2 ("dimension equality only") is **not a caveat**. $\dim_{K_0^{ur}} D_{\mathrm{pst}}(V)=\dim V$ *is* the textbook definition of potentially semistable.
- Its caveat 1 (injectivity of $B_{\mathrm{st}}\to B_{\mathrm{dR}}$ is unproved) does not affect this statement. $D_{\mathrm{pst}}$ needs only the $G_K$-ring $B_{\mathrm{st}}$, and that injectivity matters only for the converse "semistable $\Rightarrow$ de Rham".
