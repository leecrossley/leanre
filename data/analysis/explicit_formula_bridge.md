## 3. Concrete Answers for an Analytic Number Theorist

### 3.1 Exact Inequality Killing a Hypothetical Zero $\rho = \beta + i\gamma$ ($\beta > 1/2$) `[L]` `[S]`

No all-$y$ bound is used. Given any hypothetical zero $s_0$ with $\operatorname{Re}(s_0) > 1/2$, `level_of_tiedWin` chooses $\theta = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\min(\operatorname{Re}(s_0) - 1/2, 1/2)}{2} \in (\frac{1}{2}, \operatorname{Re}(s_0))$ and shrinks $\varepsilon$ so that $(e_D + e_L + 7)\varepsilon \le \theta - \frac{1}{2}$. The **only** analytic inequality needed to kill every zero $\rho$ with $\operatorname{Re} \rho > \theta$ is the **one-sided windowed upper bound** ([formB](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/PrimitiveCosets/Imports.lean#L8), [windowHyp_of_rpow](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/WindowHypOfRpow.lean#L27)):
$$\forall Y_0,\; \exists Y \ge Y_0,\; \forall y \in [Y, Y^{3/2}],\qquad \mathrm{Gm}_\Lambda(y) = G_z(y) + \sum_{\psi \in \Lambda.\mathrm{Nt}} \mathrm{wt}_\Lambda(\psi)\,\operatorname{Re} g_\chi(\psi.\chi, y) \le y^\theta.$$
In logarithmic coordinates $u = \log y$, $g(u) = \mathrm{Gm}_\Lambda(e^u)$, this gives $g(u) \le R(u) := e^{\theta u}$ on $[U, (1+\delta)U]$ ($\delta = 1/2$, $U = \log Y$) with $\int_U^{(1+\delta)U} R(u)e^{-\theta u}\,du = \delta U \le e^{\eta U}$ for all $\eta > 0$ and large $U$.

How that one-sided window bound kills $\rho$ in [landau_window](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/landauWindow.lean#L83):
