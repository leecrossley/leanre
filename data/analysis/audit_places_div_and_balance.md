## 2. Unpacking `ch_balance` `[L][S]`

Write $G=E_0\times H$, $\Phi=\{g:g_{1,1}=0\}$ (index $2$, $|\Phi|=f$), $g^\ast=$ [partner](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L19) $=\mathrm{cconj}+g+\mathrm{top}(t)$, and $\chi_C$ = [chC](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L21) (the sign character of $\Phi$). The proof has three steps.

- **Step A, [askExp](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChBalance.lean#L122).** It rewrites `ASK` as $\frac1f\sum_{g\notin\Phi}\sum_\psi\overline{\psi(g)}\sum_{n\le N}\Lambda(n)\,\mathrm{pv}_\psi(n)\,(a/n-w(g^\ast))_+$. Here [primVal](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L65) is the inertia-coset mean of $\psi(i\,\mathrm{Frob}_p+\cdot)$. `[S]` By orthogonality $\frac1f\sum_\psi\overline{\psi(g)}\psi(h)=\frac{|G|}{f}\mathbf 1_{g=h}=2\cdot\mathbf 1_{g=h}$, which is the weight $2\log p$ of [ASK](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L84).
- **Step B, `sum_cut` and [inner_eq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/StronglyIrreducible.lean#L655).** For $W>0$ and $a/W\le N$,
$$\sum_{n\le N}\Lambda(n)\,\mathrm{pv}_\psi(n)\Bigl(\frac an-W\Bigr)_+=W\,G_\psi\!\Bigl(\frac aW\Bigr)+\begin{cases}a\log a-a\log W-(1+\gamma)a&\psi=0,\\-a\,\ell(\psi)&\psi\ne0,\end{cases}$$
which is the definition of [GPsi](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L68) (with [Gz](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/StatementOC.lean#L13) $=\sum\Lambda(n)(x/n-1)-x\log x+(1+\gamma)x$ at $\psi=0$) multiplied out.
- **Step C, [ColourSum](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L123).** It states $\sum_{g\notin\Phi}\overline{\psi(g)}=f$ if $\psi=0$, $-f$ if $\psi=\chi_C$, and $0$ otherwise. So every polar term $-a\,\ell(\psi)$ with $\psi\ne0,\chi_C$ cancels, $\psi=\chi_C$ leaves $+f\,a\,\ell(\chi_C)$, and `sum_partner` turns $\sum_{g\notin\Phi}\log w(g^\ast)$ into $\sum_{\rho\in\Phi}\log w(\rho)$. Dividing by $f$ gives exactly [MAIN](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L89), and the $G_\psi$ terms assemble into [De](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L93).

**Where analysis enters, and why there is no loophole `[L][M]`.** The only consumer is [core](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChCore.lean#L283) (`dom` `66` for `ch_balance`, fan-in `1`). There [th_le](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChCore.lean#L75) splits the samples in `De`:

- Thick classes have $w\in[b,b+\eta+1]$ and sample $G_\psi$ at $y\approx x$.
- Thin classes have $w\le2b'$ and sample at $y\ge X'/3$, where `ThinScales` gives $|G_\psi(y)|\le y\theta$. The cost is $2f_{\mathrm{Pl}}(x+1)b\theta$.

Averaging over translates $u_0\in A_0$ then isolates $\frac{m\,b\,\delta_2}{2}\sum_t\operatorname{Re}\sum_{\psi\in\Psi_A}\psi(e_1t)\,G_\psi(a_t/b)$ with a *positive* coefficient. The coefficients $\overline{\psi(g)}\,w(g^\ast)$ are fixed by the group. The thresholds $w$ are the only freedom, and `SizesA` constrains them. `char_at` sets $\ell(\psi)=\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi^{\mathrm{prim}}_\psi)$ for **every** $\psi$ and $\operatorname{Re}\ell(\chi_C)$ = `L.logDerivativeAtOne`. Its thin clause is conditional on [ChThin_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/Chosen.lean#L47): $|G_\chi(y)|\le y\,e^{-c\,x^{\varepsilon/8}}$ for $y\ge e^{x^{\varepsilon/3}}/3$. That is a saving $\exp(-c(\log y)^{3/8})$, classical in strength `[S]`. `ChThin_at` is supplied through `chosenThin_of_boxesN` from `LitThin`, proved by [litThin](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/GlobalInterpolation/LitThin.lean#L8). `LitThin` states (i) at most $C_1Q^{1/4}$ zeros with $\operatorname{Re}\rho\ge1-\frac1{400}$, $|\operatorname{Im}\rho|\le Q$, conductor $\le Q$, and (ii) a de la Vallée Poussin region with at most one real simple exceptional zero.

## 3. `Places_at`: How Local Gains Become a Slope of $N$ `[L][S]`

[Places_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/TopDensityNGeSixteen.lean#L1516) bundles [HypLow](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ClaimGaFree.lean#L65), [HypLow2](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ClaimGaFree.lean#L80) and [HypHigh](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ClaimGaFree.lean#L94) with two error caps, $LT\le C\,n_Tm(a/k+b')\log T$ and $RX\le C\,X''$. Its producer [places_at_of](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GlobalTheory/PlacesP.lean#L297) (`dom` `24`) rests on three lattice facts.

- [mu_diff](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/AdmissibleParameterJoins/SumLogPrimeFactorsLe.lean#L380) and [mu_eq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GlobalTheory/PlacesP.lean#L129) give an exact degree change:
$$\mu(N_u)=\mu(M^H_u)+\sum_{w\in\mathrm{supp}}2\ell_w\bigl\lfloor\mathrm{ur}_w(\mathrm{sg}_{u,w})\bigr\rfloor-\frac{1}{\operatorname{rk}N_u}\sum_{w\in\mathrm{supp}}2\ell_w\,e_w\,\mathrm{len}_{MN}(u,w).$$
`[S]` This is the Arakelov degree formula for a lattice modified at finitely many finite places (product formula; $\ell_w=\log$ of the residue size).
- [muMax_le](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/LocalToGlobal/GlobalTheory/PlacesP.lean#L171) gives the analogous upper bound for $\mu_{\max}$.
- The local length bound is $\mathrm{len}_{MN}\le d_u(\tau_w+io_w)$ off the level prime.

[slopesN_at_of](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/TopDensityNGeSixteen.lean#L1529) (`dom` `16`) then rewrites $\sum_j(\mathrm{gain}+F-E)$ as $\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}$ (`Ga_eq`, `Fo_eq`, `Ee_eq'`). This yields [SlopesN_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/QuadraticLogDerivCongr.lean#L181), i.e. row (Q): $\mu(M_u)+\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}(u)-\mathrm{Lo}(u)\le\mu(N_u)$, together with $\mu_{\max}(N_u)\le\mu_{\max}(M_u)+\mathrm{Ga}+\mathrm{Fo}-\mathrm{Ee}+Cm B_0$. **The gains push $\mu(N_u)$ up.** Nothing here bounds anything from above.

**The global positivity that closes the chain `[L][S]`.** Row (S) is [SlopeIneq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/RankGapBounds/MeanBoundSecondForm.lean#L151), filled from [Frame_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/QuadraticLogDerivCongr.lean#L188). Its producer [frame_at_of](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/TopDensityNGeSixteen.lean#L538) (`dom` `212`) ends in [joint_a_ramified_holds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/ChainSlopeWindows/JointDModelAppliesUnitLattice.lean#L254) (`dom` `208`), which proves `JointARam`:
$$\underbrace{\frac{\sum_u d_u(\mu(N_u)-h(u))}{\sum_u d_u}}_{s}-\log|W|-\frac{4u_F\log(1+HH/u_F)+(\eta+4\sqrt e)\,HH}{1-\delta}\ \le\ \mu(RR^\vee).$$
The hypotheses are [LatGeom](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FrameTraceRatioFinEquiv.lean#L59):

- integral maps $B_u:N_u\to RR^\vee$;
- archimedean heights $\mathrm{ht}(B_u)\le h(u)$;
- over a totally complex $K$, a near-isometric frame at one complex place ($\mathrm{Ball}=B_f V_r$ with $V_r^\ast V_r=1$, Gram defects $\eta_0,\eta_1,e_0$).

`[S]` This is the averaged form of Bost's slope inequality $\mu_{\min}(E)\le\mu_{\max}(F)+h(\varphi)$, which the project also states for a single map as [HN.SlopeIneq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/HarderNarasimhan/Statement.lean#L91). The price of using $|W|$ maps is the entropy term $\log|W|\le(k-3)\log2$. **So `ASK ≤ Main + Err` is forced downward because:**

- (L) puts $\mathrm{ASK}$ below $\mathrm{Ga}$;
- (Q) puts $\mathrm{Ga}$ below $\mu(N_u)-\mu(M_u)$;
- (S) caps the weighted mean of $\mu(N_u)-h(u)$ by $\mu(RR^\vee)$;
- (D) caps $\mu(RR^\vee)+h(u)-\mathrm{Fo}$ by the explicit CM main term (§4);
- `Slopes` bounds $|\mu(M_u)|\le c\,n_Ta\,L_a(1+m/k)$.

Each link is one-directional `[M]`.
