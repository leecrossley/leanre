> **Target:** `LinkG` (`linkG_concrete5`, `dom = 10,772`), rank-`m` error scaling in `Count5`/`DIV`/`Slopes`, and `key_identity` main-term matching (follows [the RH reading guide](./rh_proof_exposition.md) §4.2–4.3; pinned `40e56bab`)

## 1. Verdicts

1. **`LinkG` is a per-prime bookkeeping inequality, not a global prime-distribution estimate `[L][M]`.** Every conjunct of [LinkG_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalTheoremInputs.lean#L122) is quantified $\forall p$ (resp. $\forall w$). The "local gains" it bounds against are *defined* from the same truncated prime-power terms $(a/p^e-c)_+$ over Frobenius orbits. The proof is a geometric-series identity, threshold shifts and orbit re-indexing. It uses no Chebotarev, explicit-formula or trace-formula input. Nothing is weakened (the constant is $4|C|+1$, uniform in $x$), but `LinkG` cannot carry the strength of the final bound. That strength sits in (a) the exact orthogonality identity $\mathrm{ASK}-\mathrm{MAIN}=\mathrm{De}$, (b) [Places_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalSchurBounds/TopDensityNGeSixteen.lean#L1516), which turns gains into slope of $N$, and (c) the DIV clause of [LinkDIV_at](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SharpRestriction/GlobalTheoremInputs.lean#L77).
2. **The no-factor-$m$ hinge holds in the statements and is forced by their quantifier shape `[L]`.** The three $m$-free error terms all have the form $c\,n_T\,a\,L_a$ (from `Count5`, `DIV` and `Slopes`). In each of them $c$ is fixed by $\exists C\,x_0\,\forall x\ge x_0\,\forall i$ *before* $x$ is chosen, while $m=m(x,i)\to\infty$. So no constant can hide $m$. The inputs $a=xb$, $L_a=\log(a\cdot\mathrm{rd}_E)\le2x^\varepsilon$, $b$ and $n_T$ contain no $m$. **Correction to the guide:** $B_0=f\,m\,b\,x^\varepsilon=2n\,m\,b\,x^\varepsilon$ *itself* contains $m$, so the defect terms $m(B_0+N_c)$ grow like $m^2$. Also $N_c\propto k$, and $k\ge xm$. Both effects match the error bracket.
3. **Main terms match exactly, with no fudge term and no sign flip `[L]`.** [key_identity](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/RankGapBounds/RankGapProofs/WeightedMeanData.lean#L170) cancels $\log(\pi/v)$ and $m\,\mathrm{sumA}\,\mathrm{lg}$ by an exact `linear_combination`. [ch_top_main_eq](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/LandauTheorems/ChTop.lean#L11) proves $\mathrm{Main}(u_0)=\sum_{t,j}\operatorname{Re}\mathrm{MAIN}$, with $\gamma$ and $\operatorname{Re}\ell(\chi_C)$ fixed by [IdentU](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/DedekindLFunctions/SplitInertia.lean#L67).
4. **Hygiene is clean `[K]`.** The cone has only the 3 standard axioms, `0` `sorryAx` / `ofReduceBool` / `ofReduceNat` / `trustCompiler`, `0` project opaques and `0` `decide_kernel`. Two benign drops are listed in §6.

$$\mathrm{gainP}_j(p)=\sum_{t_0\in VV}\max\bigl(g_U-\mathrm{Pr},\,g_0,\,0\bigr)+\sum_{t_0\in\mathrm{reps}\setminus VV}\max(g_0,0),\qquad g_0=\sum_{s\in\mathrm{tops}(t_0)}\sum_{\substack{e\ \mathrm{odd}\\ \neg\mathrm{Excl}}}\Bigl(\frac{al_s}{p^e}-\mathrm{cap}_U\Bigr)_+-\mathrm{dl},$$

## 5. Main-Term Matching in `DIV` / `Count5` `[L]`

With $\log\Omega=\gamma+\log(\pi/v)-\ell$, `DIV` bounds $\mu_{CC}+h-\mathrm{Fo}$ by $\sum_t\bigl[m(\mathrm{ph}(a_t)-a_t\log\Omega)-a_t\log T\bigr]$ plus errors, where $\mathrm{ph}(y)=y\log y-y$. Inserting the `Count5` lower bound for $\log T$:

$$-a_t\log T\le-a_t\sum_{j}\frac1f\sum_\rho\log w_{j\rho}-m\,a_t(\log v-\log\pi)-m\,a_t\,\mathrm{lg}+a_t\,c(L_a+m^2L_a/k).$$

- The $\log(\pi/v)$ terms cancel exactly: each $j$-sum has $f$ terms weighted $1/f$, and $f>0$ by `HidaSetting`.
- The term $-m\,\mathrm{sumA}\,\mathrm{lg}$, with $\mathrm{lg}=\log(g_0g_1)$ for the two level primes, cancels against $\mathrm{Ee}$ in (Q), because `EComp5` forces $|\mathrm{Ee}-m\,\mathrm{sumA}\,\mathrm{lg}|\le c_E\,m\,B_0$.
- The sign is consistent: $+m\,\mathrm{lg}$ sits on the lower-bound side of $\log T$ and $-\mathrm{Ee}$ enters (Q).

What remains is exactly [Data.Main](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/RankGapBounds/MeanBoundSecondForm.lean#L107), $\sum_j\sum_t a_t\bigl(\log a_t-1-\mathrm{gam}+\mathrm{LpL}-\frac1f\sum_\rho\log w_{j\rho}\bigr)$, which is `key_identity` up to the displayed error. `IdentU` pins $\mathrm{gam}=\gamma$, $\mathrm{LpL}=\operatorname{Re}\ell(\chi_C)$, $a_t$ and $w$ to the character-sum data, and `ASK` to $\sum_{t,j}$ [ASK/MAIN](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Formalism/SizeWindowBrackets/FirstPrimeNeSecondPrime.lean#L89). There $\mathrm{MAIN}=a\bigl(\log a-1-\gamma+\ell(\chi_C)-\frac1{|\Phi|}\sum\log w\bigr)$. `[S]` Sign check: $\sum_{n\le y}\Lambda(n)/n=\log y-\gamma+o(1)$ gives the polar term $y(\log y-1-\gamma)$ of $G_1$. Inert weighting $1-\chi_C$ adds $+\ell(\chi_C)$ because $\sum\Lambda\chi_C/n\to-\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_C)$. A threshold $w$ samples $G$ at $y=a/w$, which gives $-\log w$. Only $\operatorname{Re}$ is used, harmless for real $\chi_C$ `[M]`.
