## 2. TB: the cell-system theorem

### 2.1 Logical form

The three headline theorems quantify over a size `k`, an index type `C`, two counts `nE`, `nM`, and seven records `D`, `X`, `R`, `T`, `Y`, `N`, `B`, and assert that `CellSystemCondition D X R T Y N B` implies `BreakdownOnSpace`, `BreakdownOnTorus`, or both [`Solution.lean#L338-L355`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Solution.lean#L338-L355). Both conclusions are closed propositions: for every `ν > 0` there is a smooth, divergence-free datum (compactly supported, respectively unit-periodic) for which the unforced equations have no smooth solution on `[0, ∞) × ℝ³` with bounded energy (respectively with unit-periodic velocity) [`Challenge.lean#L35-L45`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Challenge.lean#L35-L45). The theorem therefore has the form "(there exist data with the condition) ⟹ breakdown", and its content rests entirely on whether the hypothesis is inhabited.

### 2.2 Is the condition inhabited?

**No inhabitant exists in the repository. Verified.**

- Outside the fractional files, the identifier occurs on 24 lines of 7 files: 2 are the two copies of the definition and the other 22 are hypothesis binders of the form `(h : CellSystemCondition …)`. No line constructs a term of this type: the field names `shape_valueSets` and `entry_conditions` never appear on the left of `:=`, there is no `CellSystemCondition.mk`, no `Nonempty`, no `∃` over it, and the tree contains no `example`.
- The only theorem that produces the intermediate certificate predicate does so from the condition itself: `isExtendedCertificate_of_parts` [`CellLayerBreakdownCompose.lean#L171-L179`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Breakdown/CellReduction/CellLayerBreakdownCompose.lean#L171-L179). Every theorem concluding `BreakdownOnSpace` or `BreakdownOnTorus` has a hypothesis.
- Upstream says the same: "The formalization contains no data" [`README.md#L36-L39`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/README.md#L36-L39), and for this file "No such data are given in this package" [`NOTICE#L17`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/NOTICE#L17).
- The tree has no `axiom`, `opaque`, `native_decide`, or `sorry` outside the statement files (§3), so the hypothesis is not discharged by a hidden assumption either. It is an explicit parameter of the theorem.

Consequence: within Lean the headline result is "Appendix B conditions ⟹ breakdown". The step "the published certificate satisfies the conditions" is an interval-arithmetic computation that is not in the repository and has no formal link to the `Prop` (the reals in the records are Lean reals, not enclosures). **Unresolved** from the repository.

### 2.5 Pinned constants and the smallness they force

Field 1 fixes four constants outright: the weight function is identically `39/50`, the coercivity is `3/50`, the pin weight is `1000`, and `rowWeightMax = 400·(1 + 2⁻¹⁸)` [`StatementDefs.lean#L4391-L4397`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Breakdown/CertCell/StatementDefs.lean#L4391-L4397). With field 2 this gives `w = 39/50` for the weight. The following consequences hold for **any** inhabitant; they are hand derivations from the cited clauses, not machine-checked (**Verified** by reading, arithmetic rounded outward).

1. `rowBound ≥ √(rowWeightMax / c) ≈ 81.65`, from the row-instance clause `rowWeightMax / c ≤ rowBound²` [`StatementDefs.lean#L1534-L1541`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Breakdown/CertCell/StatementDefs.lean#L1534-L1541).
2. `coercivePin ≥ √(1000 / (3/50)) ≈ 129.1`, from field 6.

First, these are necessary conditions of the right kind: any inhabitant is a numerically very accurate approximate profile, so the hypothesis is not satisfiable by a trivial or crude object. Second, the non-triviality clause only asks `4·sizeBound²/w < ‖∇(profile)‖²` over a period [`StatementDefs.lean#L6162-L6163`](https://github.com/tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes/blob/ad53419de079eb8f95f60313473374624a348546/lean/Breakdown/CertCell/StatementDefs.lean#L6162-L6163); because the right side is the real part of an extended integral, the clause also forces that integral to be finite and positive.

### 2.7 The Comparator boundary for the cell layer

Comparator pairs a reference module, trusted for its statements, with a solution module. ## 8. Method

- Token counts are extended-regular-expression counts over every `.lean` file at the pinned commits, with word boundaries for `sorry`, `admit`, `opaque`, `unsafe`, `partial` and `decide`, and with hits in comments separated by reading where the count is small.
- Import closures follow `import` lines inside each project.
- Clause counts parse the `Prop`-valued structures of the 19 modules imported by the cell-layer reference and follow capitalized declaration names, together with lowercase definition names that are not also field names. Dot-notation projections are not followed, so the closure figures are lower bounds; the per-structure clause counts are exact.
- Copy comparisons are exact text comparisons of whole declarations after trimming trailing whitespace.
- Every link in this note points to a pinned blob; each cited line range was checked by script to exist and to contain the identifier or phrase it is cited for.
